TCG Calendar Hub

How we use your details

Last updated 25 November 2026. This notice covers the free testing version of the TCG Calendar Hub app.

Who we are

TCG Calendar Hub. The app is published on the App Store by PowerFuel Performance Ltd. Contact: tcgcalendarhub@outlook.com.

What we keep on our server, so we can alert you

  • Your alert choices: your country, the towns you picked and how far you will travel, your games and event types, and which alerts you have switched on. Towns are stored as the town you picked (its name and map point), never your home address or live location.
  • Your phone's time zone, so alerts arrive in the daytime.
  • A code for sending push alerts to your phone, the type of phone (iPhone or Android) and the app version.
  • A random ID for your phone. There are no accounts or passwords, and it is not linked to your name or email.
  • A record of which alerts we sent, so you never get the same show twice.

What stays on your phone only

  • Any name or email you add, the shows you mark as going, and your card collection. We cannot see them.
  • If you add a show to your calendar, the app writes that one event to the calendar you choose. It does not read your calendar.

What we use it for

Only to send you the alerts you have switched on, about shows that match your towns, games and event types. No advertising, no tracking across other apps, and we never sell or share it.

Who helps us

Supabase stores the server data, in London. Expo sends the push alerts through Apple and Google. They only process it for us.

How long we keep it

While you use the app. Delete my data in the app's Settings removes everything on your phone and your record on our server straight away, including your alert history. If a phone stops using the app, we delete its record after 12 months.

Links to organisers

Ticket and organiser links open the organiser's own website or ticket seller. Their own privacy notice applies there.

Your rights

  • You can ask what we hold, ask us to correct or delete it, or object, by emailing tcgcalendarhub@outlook.com, or use Delete my data in the app.
  • UK users can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
  • The app is free. There are no payments.
