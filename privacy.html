TCG Calendar Hub

How we use your details

Last updated 25 November 2026. This notice covers the free testing version of the TCG Calendar Hub app.

Who we are

TCG Calendar Hub. The app is published on the App Store by PowerFuel Performance Ltd. Contact: tcgcalendarhub@outlook.com.

What we collect

  • Nothing leaves your phone in this version of the app. Your towns, distances, games, saved shows, collection and any name or email you add are kept on your phone only. We cannot see them.
  • Your towns are stored as the town you picked, never your home address or live location.
  • If you add a show to your calendar, the app writes that one event to the calendar you choose. It does not read your calendar.
  • If you allow notifications, the app uses them for alerts and reminders on your phone.
  • The app is free. There are no payments, accounts or passwords.

Links to organisers

Ticket and organiser links open the organiser's own website or ticket seller. Their own privacy notice applies there.

Your rights

  • Delete my data in the app's Settings removes everything the app has stored on your phone. Deleting the app does the same.
  • If anything changes, for example when alerts start coming from our server, we will update this notice and tell you in the app before it happens.
  • You can contact us about your data at tcgcalendarhub@outlook.com. UK users can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
