Support

Questions, problems or a show we have missed? Email tcgcalendarhub@outlook.com. We reply within two working days.

Common questions

Is the app free?
Yes. It is free while we test, with everything unlocked.

A show is missing or has the wrong details.
Email us the show name, date and the organiser's own page or ticket link, and we will add or correct it. Organisers can email us too.

How do I change my towns, distance or games?
Open Settings in the app and tap Edit next to My areas or What I collect, then Save changes.

How do I add a show to my calendar?
Open the show and tap Add to my calendar. You choose which calendar it goes in.

How do I delete my data?
Open Settings and tap Delete my data. In this version everything is stored on your phone only, so this removes it all. Deleting the app does the same.
